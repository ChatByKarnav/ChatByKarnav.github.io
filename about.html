Meet ChatByKarnav.

ChatByKarnav is a web messaging project created by Karnav Gandhi for friends and classmates to stay connected through conversations.

Open ChatByKarnavExplore beta access

What is ChatByKarnav?

ChatByKarnav brings a familiar messaging experience to the web. The project focuses on making it straightforward to connect, find conversations and communicate with people you know. Access to some features may depend on account status and the current release.

Built around conversations

Private conversations

Start one-to-one conversations with other members when your account has access.

Group communication

Keep group discussions together in a shared conversation space.

Member experience

Use your account profile and available messaging tools in the web app.

Beta access

Interested in trying the beta? Visit the beta area to read the current information and submit an application. Applications are reviewed manually; submitting one does not guarantee access.
