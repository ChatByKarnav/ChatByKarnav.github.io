Support & Contact

Send a question, report a problem or share an idea. Your message will be sent to the ChatByKarnav support inbox.

Privacy reminder: Never include your password, one-time codes, private chat messages, payment information or sensitive documents. Only include information needed to understand your issue.

What happens next?

Your submission is forwarded to the project support inbox. A reply is not automatic; responses depend on availability. Please do not submit the same request repeatedly.
