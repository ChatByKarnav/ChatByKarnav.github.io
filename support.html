Support & Contact

Send a question, report a problem or share an idea. Your message will be saved securely and reviewed in the ChatByKarnav Admin Portal.

Privacy reminder: Never include your password, one-time codes, private chat messages, payment information or sensitive documents. Only include information needed to understand your issue.

What happens next?

Your request will appear in the private Admin Portal. Replies are not automatic; include a correct email address if you need a response.
