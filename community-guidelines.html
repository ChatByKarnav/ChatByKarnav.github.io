Community Guidelines

Last updated: 26 November 2026

These guidelines apply to profiles, private conversations, group conversations and other areas of ChatByKarnav. The goal is to help friends and classmates communicate respectfully.

Be respectful

  • Do not bully, harass, threaten, intimidate or repeatedly target anyone.
  • Respect differences, boundaries and requests to stop contacting someone.
  • Do not impersonate another person or mislead people about who you are.

Keep people safe

  • Do not share another person's private information, images or conversations without permission.
  • Never request or share passwords, verification codes, financial details or highly sensitive information.
  • Do not share sexual, exploitative or otherwise harmful content involving minors.
  • Do not use the service for scams, spam, threats, hate, malware or illegal activity.

Use the service responsibly

Do not attempt to access accounts, conversations or systems without authorization, bypass restrictions, disrupt the service or misuse its features.

Reporting concerns

If you experience or see a violation, use the Report Abuse page or contact support. Share only the information needed to explain the concern. Do not publish sensitive evidence publicly.

Enforcement

Reports may be reviewed when possible. Depending on the circumstances and available tools, action may include a warning, content removal, feature restriction or account suspension. We cannot promise that every report will be reviewed immediately or that every harmful interaction can be prevented.

These guidelines may be updated as the project evolves. They do not replace applicable law or emergency services.
