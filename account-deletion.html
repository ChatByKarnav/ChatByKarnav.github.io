Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 26 November 2026

You can request deletion of your ChatByKarnav account and ask for associated personal information to be erased, subject to applicable law and any limited retention required for security, legal or operational reasons.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Support & Contact page.
  2. Choose the topic Account or data deletion.
  3. Send the email address associated with the account and state that you want the account deleted. Do not send your password or verification codes.

Verification and processing

We may ask you to verify that you control the account or are legally authorized to make the request. This helps prevent someone else from deleting your account. A request is not completed merely by submitting the form; wait for confirmation from support.

What deletion may affect

Deletion may remove or disable account access and may affect profile information, messages or other content associated with the account. Some information may remain where retention is required or permitted by law, or where it is necessary to protect security and resolve disputes. Content already received or copied by other users may remain outside our control.

Before requesting

Account deletion may be permanent and may not be reversible. If you only need help signing in or changing account details, contact support first.

Important: This page describes the current request route through support. It does not mean that an automatic self-service deletion button is available in the app.
