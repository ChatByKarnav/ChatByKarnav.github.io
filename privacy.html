Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how ChatByKarnav handles information when you visit or use the ChatByKarnav website and messaging features. ChatByKarnav is a community messaging project created by Karnav Gandhi.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: information you provide when registering or managing an account, such as your email address, display name and profile details.
  • Messages and shared content: messages, group conversations, profile content and files or other content you choose to submit through the app.
  • Support requests: your name, email address, selected topic and message when you use the support/contact form.
  • Notification information: browser/device notification permission and subscription identifiers or related information needed to deliver push notifications, if you enable them.
  • Technical information: information automatically processed by the website or its service providers to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service, such as basic request, browser and diagnostic data.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, provide messaging and profile features, deliver notifications you enable, respond to support requests, maintain security, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors and improve the service.

3. Service providers

ChatByKarnav uses third-party services to operate parts of the website. Appwrite is used for backend functions such as account and application data services. OneSignal is used for web push notifications when you choose to enable them. The support form is processed through FormSubmit to forward submissions to the project support inbox. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Messages and visibility

Messages and content are intended for the conversation or feature in which you share them. Do not post passwords, sign-in codes, payment details or other highly sensitive information. Do not assume that anything you submit online is absolutely confidential or that recipients cannot copy or share it.

5. Notifications and your choices

Push notifications require browser/device permission. You can manage or revoke notification permission in your browser or device settings. Disabling notifications may stop push alerts but does not necessarily delete your account or messages.

6. Sharing and disclosure

ChatByKarnav does not offer your personal information for sale. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, disclosed when required by applicable law, or used when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users or the public. Information you choose to share with other users is visible according to the relevant app feature and its access settings.

7. Storage, security and retention

Information is stored or processed by ChatByKarnav and its service providers for as long as needed to operate the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with applicable requirements. No website or online storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not use ChatByKarnav to store information that requires guaranteed confidentiality or availability.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose what profile information and content you submit, and you can turn off browser notifications. For questions or requests concerning your account information, contact us through the Support & Contact page. We may need to verify a request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal or operational reasons.

9. Children and appropriate use

ChatByKarnav is a community project. If you are under the age at which you can independently consent to online services where you live, use the service only with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required. Do not share another person's personal information without permission.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. Continued use after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, use the ChatByKarnav support form.
