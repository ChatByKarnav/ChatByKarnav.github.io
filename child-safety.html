Child Safety & Parental Consent

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ChatByKarnav is a community messaging project intended for friends and classmates. Online communication can involve risks, and the service does not guarantee that every interaction is safe.

Age and permission

Use ChatByKarnav only if you are legally permitted to do so where you live. If you are below the age at which you can independently consent to online services or data processing under applicable law, do not register or use the service unless the required parent or legal guardian involvement and consent have been obtained. This page does not set a universal minimum age or replace local legal requirements.

Safety rules

  • Never share your password, one-time codes, home address, school timetable or live location in public or group spaces.
  • Do not agree to meet someone you only know online without involving a trusted adult.
  • Tell a parent, guardian or trusted adult if someone makes you uncomfortable, threatens you or asks for private images or information.
  • Do not create, request, store or share sexual or exploitative content involving anyone under 18.

Reporting child-safety concerns

Report grooming, exploitation, threats, impersonation or other safety concerns through Report Abuse. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services and a trusted adult; do not rely on this website for urgent assistance.

Our limitations

ChatByKarnav is a small project and does not claim to provide continuous human moderation, age verification or emergency response. Do not assume that all users have been verified or that all content is monitored.

Parents and guardians

Parents and guardians should discuss online safety with children and supervise use where appropriate. To ask a question or raise a concern about a child's account or information, contact support. We may need reasonable information to verify authority before discussing or acting on an account.
