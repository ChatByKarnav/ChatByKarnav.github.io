Report Abuse

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Use this page to report conduct or content that may violate the Community Guidelines or put someone at risk.

What you can report

  • Bullying, harassment, threats or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Impersonation, scams, spam or malicious links.
  • Sharing private information or images without permission.
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming or other child-safety concerns.
  • Unauthorized access, account abuse or other security concerns.

How to report

Go to Support & Contact and select Report abuse or safety concern. Include the relevant username or profile, approximate date/time, a short description and any context needed to understand the issue. Do not include passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary personal information.

Urgent danger

ChatByKarnav is not an emergency service and reports are not monitored continuously. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services and a trusted adult or appropriate authority.

What happens after a report?

Reports may be reviewed when support is available. We may request clarification and may take action consistent with our terms and available technical controls. We cannot guarantee a response time, a particular outcome or that all reported content can be removed.

Please make reports honestly and do not use the reporting process to target or harass others.
