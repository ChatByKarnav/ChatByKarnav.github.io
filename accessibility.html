Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ChatByKarnav aims to make its website understandable and usable by as many people as reasonably possible. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we do not currently claim that every page or feature conforms to a particular accessibility standard.

Current approach

  • Use semantic page headings, links, labels and form controls where practical.
  • Provide keyboard focus indicators for key interactive controls.
  • Use responsive layouts intended to work on different screen sizes.
  • Use readable text and color contrast as design goals, while recognizing that issues may remain.

Known limitations

Some interactive messaging features, third-party components, dynamically updated content or visual controls may not work equally well with every assistive technology or input method. We have not completed a formal independent accessibility audit.

Feedback and help

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact support. Tell us which page or feature you were using, what you were trying to do and what assistive technology or browser details are relevant. Do not include sensitive personal information.

We will consider reasonable improvements as the project develops.
